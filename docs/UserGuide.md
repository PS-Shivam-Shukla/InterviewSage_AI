# User Guide

## Getting Started

### 1. Create an account

Go to `http://localhost:5173` and click **Get started**. Enter your name, email, and a password (minimum 8 characters).

### 2. Upload your resume

After logging in, click **New interview** on your dashboard.

- Drag and drop your PDF, DOCX, or plain-text resume onto the upload area, or click **browse files**.
- The system extracts your skills, experience, and infers a seniority level (JUNIOR / MID / SENIOR / STAFF).

### 3. Paste the job description

On the next screen:
- Enter the **Target role** (e.g. "Senior Backend Engineer").
- Optionally add the **Company name** — this enables company-specific question calibration.
- Paste the full job description text into the large text area.
- Click **Continue**.

### 4. Review the interview plan

The wizard shows you the planned interview structure:
- **HR Round** — behavioral questions (typically 4–5, based on your seniority).
- **Technical Round** — role-specific questions (typically 6–8).
- Estimated duration.

Click **Begin interview** when ready.

### 5. The live interview

The interview runs question by question:

- Each question appears in the **Question Card** at the top, tagged with the competency it targets and its difficulty level.
- Type your answer in the text box. Take as long as you need — there is no time limit.
- Press **Submit answer** (or Ctrl+Enter / ⌘+Enter).
- The AI evaluates your answer immediately. You will see:
  - Your score for that answer (1–10).
  - A brief rubric breakdown.
  - Specific feedback.
- The next question then streams in word by word.

If you need to pause, close the browser. Your progress is checkpointed automatically — resume by returning to the same interview URL.

### 6. The report

When all questions are answered, the system generates your full report:

- **Overall score** — weighted average across all competencies.
- **Executive summary** — a 2–3 sentence description of your performance.
- **Competency scorecard** — score per competency visualised as a progress bar and radar chart.
- **Improvement plan** — prioritised, specific action items. Every item cites the exact question and answer that revealed the gap.
- **Annotated transcript** — expand any question to see your answer, the score, feedback, and the ideal-answer summary.
- **Download PDF** — exports the full report as a text file.

### 7. Dashboard and Analytics

After completing interviews, your dashboard shows:
- Summary stats (total interviews, average score, completion rate).
- Score trend line across sessions.
- Competency radar chart aggregated across all interviews.

The **Analytics** page shows detailed breakdowns. The **History** page lists all your past interviews with their scores and statuses.

## Tips

- **Be specific**: STAR-format answers (Situation, Task, Action, Result) score significantly higher on behavioral questions.
- **Think aloud on technical questions**: the evaluation agent rewards structured problem-solving, not just correct answers.
- **Use the improvement plan**: each item points to the exact answer that needs work. Re-do the interview after acting on the top 2–3 items.
